
Sea Creatures

Float in a primordial soup of varying richness.

Each cell has zero or more organelles, and can add organelles according to its genetic stock.

* Nucleus.  
  Contains genes ("functional RNA or protein" and unit of heredity).  
  Builds organelles from genes as needed.
  - Note that some traits may follow from several genes.
  
* Ribosome.  Produces proteins, which keeps the cell functioning well.

* Golgi apparatus.  Digester.

* Cytoskeleton.  Provides support.  Armor.

* Mitochondrion.  Power from glucose to ATP; signalling.

* Vacuole.  Storage.  Good for metabolizing, buoyancy, enzyme storage, chemical reactions, etc.

* Lysosome.  Destroyer - a vacuole with hydrolytic enzymes.  Waste disposal.

* Flagellum.  Locomotion and sensory.

* Chloroplast.  Traps energy from sunlight.

* Capsid: the protein shell of a virus.

* Receptors. Ways to bind to other things.
